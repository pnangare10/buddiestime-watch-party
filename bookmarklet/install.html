▶ Watch Party — Tap & Hold to Save

Drag to bookmarks bar (desktop) · Long-press to bookmark (phone)

2Open Hotstar in this browser

Go to hotstar.com, find the same movie or show, and start it.

3Tap the bookmark

Tap the "Watch Party" bookmark you just saved. A green badge will appear on screen when you're synced.

The badge shows your role: Host controls playback; Guest follows automatically. If you're joining an existing party, you'll be a Guest.
